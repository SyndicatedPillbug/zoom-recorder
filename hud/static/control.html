Set up zoom-recorder

It records your microphone and the other person's audio, and can save a transcript. It never records your screen. Works with Zoom, Teams, Discord, Slack, or any call in your browser.

1. Test your microphone

Say a few words out loud, then press Test.

Your microphone isn't being heard.

2. Where should the sound play?

Pick what you listen with (speakers or headphones). This only affects recordings — your Mac keeps playing sound normally the rest of the time. During calls macOS's own volume keys can't control the recording setup, so use the Volume menu in the menu bar.

3. Try a short recording

We'll record about ten seconds and play it back so you can hear both sides.

4. Live transcript (optional)

Shows what's being said while the call is happening.

Runs entirely on this Mac. Turbo is about 547 MiB and is downloaded once; base is a smaller speed-first fallback.

Audio is sent to this service while recording. You can turn it off any time, or use Offline mode in Settings to block all network access.

5. Start automatically at login?

Off by default. Turn it on so the 🎙 menu is always there after you log in.

Help

Common questions.

How do I record a call?
Click 🎙 in the menu bar → Start recording. When you're done, click it again → Stop recording. Your recording and transcript appear under Recordings.

Where are my recordings?
In your recordings folder (see Settings), shown under the Recordings tab.

Nobody can hear me / I can't hear them
Run Setup → Check my audio. If your microphone shows as silent, click Open Microphone settings and allow access. If system audio is missing, click Fix automatically.

Volume keys don't work during calls
That's macOS: the special audio setup has no volume of its own. Use the Volume menu in the menu bar (or the optional Karabiner mapping in karabiner/).

Bluetooth headphones cut out or go silent
When a Bluetooth headset switches to call mode, macOS changes its audio format and the recording setup can stop using it. Click 🎙 → Play sound through ▸ and pick the headset again after it connects, or use wired speakers/headphones for calls you record.

Does it record my screen?
No. It records your microphone and the other person's audio, and only sends audio to a service if you choose Online transcription.
